    First day
    
            
    Punch a tree for logs, turn them into planks and a crafting table, then make a wooden pickaxe. Build a shelter and craft torches from coal and sticks before the first night: zombies and creepers come out in the dark.
    
            
    Wilderness Bound
    
            
    Look for the autumn-colored Dappled Forest near cold regions. Its tall poplar trees come in red, orange and yellow and drop leaves as you walk under them. Collect shelf mushrooms from poplar trunks and fallen logs for stew.
    
            
    Abandoned camps sit around a poplar ringed with fences: tents, a campfire with cushions you can sit on, barrels and a chest holding an explorer map to the next camp. Sleep through the night in a one-use straw bed. Wool and concrete now come in 16 colors, with slabs and stairs.
    
            
    Inventory: click to pick up or place, right-click to split or place one, Shift-click to move quickly.
